What are YouTube Shorts?
YouTube Shorts are short, vertically formatted videos published on YouTube alongside the platform’s conventional long-form videos and live broadcasts. They have become a major part of YouTube’s overall activity: By 2026, Shorts averaged more than 200 billion views per day. Shorts have also produced record-setting uploads, including MrBeast’s “Would You Fly to Paris for a Baguette?,” which was YouTube’s most-liked upload as of 2026.

Who founded YouTube, and when?
YouTube was founded by Steve Chen, Chad Hurley, and Jawed Karim, three former employees of the American e-commerce company PayPal. It was registered on February 14, 2005, though it was initially launched as a video-based dating platform. The limited, or “beta,” version was first made available to the public in May 2005.

What specialized services does YouTube offer?
YouTube operates several specialized services in addition to its main video-sharing platform. YouTube Kids provides content intended for younger viewers, YouTube Premium removes advertisements and allows background and offline playback, and YouTube Music focuses on songs, albums, music videos, and personalized listening. YouTube Movies &amp; TV offers films and television programs for rental or purchase in supported markets, and YouTube TV is a standalone subscription-based streaming service available in the United States.
